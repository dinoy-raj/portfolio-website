/ Portrait lab · hero card experiments

Fourteen ways to say hi, I'm Dinoy

Card shapes and image treatments for the hero portrait, all made from the one photo in assets/dinoy.jpg. Each treatment was rendered with code, not AI, so the "cartoon" and "painterly" looks are approximations. A true Ghibli-style painting or a real Memoji would need an image model; any of these cards can take that file as a drop-in.

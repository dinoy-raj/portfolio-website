Another area we shouldn’t forget is the cover screen. When the device is folded, the user might want to complete a quick task without opening it. So we should think about what matters most on the cover screen.

In a music app, the user might mainly need playback controls. In a travel app, they might need a booking confirmation or a boarding pass. We don’t need to show the entire unfolded experience.

Instead, identify the most important task and make it easy to reach.

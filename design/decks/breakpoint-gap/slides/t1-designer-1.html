On foldables, designers own the posture layouts: tabletop with video up and controls down, and book posture for long reading. And when apps start running in desktop-sized windows, the interaction model gets richer.

Designers should think about tooltips and contextual fly-outs. For productivity apps, they should consider whether users can rearrange parts of their workspace. These are experiences that go beyond simply making a phone layout wider.

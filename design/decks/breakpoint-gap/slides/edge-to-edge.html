Now let’s talk about edge-to-edge. On modern Android, apps are expected to draw behind the system bars. The background can extend behind the status bar and navigation bar, but interactive content must still respect the right insets.

A background can fill the whole screen, while buttons and text stay inside the safe drawing area. This matters even more on large screens and desktop windows, where we may also have a taskbar or a caption bar.

The key is to separate the visual background from the safe area that interactive content needs.

⏱ 40s

Google has introduced a three-step quality roadmap. Think of it as a ladder. Tier 3 is Adaptive Ready: your app fills the window and doesn’t crash or lose state.

This becomes mandatory on Google Play in April 2027. In short: don’t break. Tier 2 is Adaptive Optimised: multi-pane layouts and keyboard and mouse polish, layouts that adapt with the window and use the available space effectively.

Tier 1 is Adaptive Differentiated: custom postures, picture-in-picture and stylus. Hit Tier 2 plus Tier 1, and you earn the Large Screen Badge.

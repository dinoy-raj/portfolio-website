Key Milestones on Google Play

Nov 2026

Now Live

Foldable screenshots in Play Console; Large Screen Badge applications open

Apr 2027

Tier 3 Is the Baseline

New apps and updates must meet Tier 3 and ship foldable screenshots

Aug 2027

Target API 37

No more opt-outs from orientation and resize on large screens

Five months until Tier 3 stops being optional

⏱ 45s

Now let’s look at the timeline. As of November 2026, developers can start publishing foldable screenshots through Play Console, and apps that meet the higher quality tiers can apply for the Large Screen Badge.

In April 2027, Google Play makes Tier 3 the baseline for standard apps: apps must meet the baseline adaptive quality requirements to publish new apps or updates, and there’s a foldable screenshot requirement too.

Then, in August 2027, new and updated apps must target API level 37. That matters, because API 37 removes the remaining opt-out for orientation and resizability restrictions on large displays.

So this isn’t something teams should postpone until the deadline.

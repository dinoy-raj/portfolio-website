⏱ 40s

Now let’s talk about breakpoints. Material 3 gives us five width size classes: Compact up to 600dp, Medium up to 840dp, Expanded up to 1200dp, and Large and Extra-large for desktops and external displays.

Below 600dp we’re in Compact: one pane and a bottom navigation bar. Watch how the structure itself transforms as we cross each threshold, not just the pixel width.

A foldable might be Compact when folded and Expanded when unfolded. A tablet might be Expanded in landscape but Medium in portrait. The important point: our app should respond to the available window, not the device model.

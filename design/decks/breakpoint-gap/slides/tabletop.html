Tabletop Mode

Content lives on the top half

Controls move to the bottom half

Perfect for video, camera, calls and maps

Design it as two layouts that share a seam
⏱ 40s

Let’s look at tabletop mode. Imagine watching a video. Traditionally, the video and the playback controls are part of the same screen. But when the foldable is partially open, we can use the top half for the video and the bottom half for the controls.

That’s more comfortable, because the device itself provides a natural separation. The same idea applies to camera previews, calls and even maps. The important thing is to design the two regions as one connected experience, rather than simply splitting the existing screen in half.

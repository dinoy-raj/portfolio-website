Meet the Postures

Flat

Fully open, one big canvas

Folded

Closed, on the cover screen

Tabletop

Half-open, hinge horizontal

Book

Half-open, hinge vertical
⏱ 30s

Foldable devices introduce something traditional phones didn’t really give us: different physical postures. Flat means the device is fully open. Folded means we’re using the outer cover screen.

Tabletop means the device is partially open with the hinge horizontal. And book posture means it’s partially open with the hinge vertical. These postures create different opportunities. Tabletop mode can be useful for video calls, while book posture can give a more natural reading experience.

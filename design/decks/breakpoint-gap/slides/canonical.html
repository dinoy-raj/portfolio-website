Here are three layout patterns designers should become familiar with: Material 3’s canonical layouts, proven and standardised patterns designed to adapt. The important thing is that these aren’t tablet-specific layouts.

They are reusable patterns that respond to the space available. The first is list-detail. Think about an email app: the inbox on one side and the selected email on the other.

Hotel bookings and contacts work the same way. The second is a feed: a collection of cards in one or more columns. On a phone we might show one column; as the window expands, we add columns.

Think news, shopping or travel activities. The third is a supporting pane: the primary content stays the focus while secondary information appears alongside it. Imagine a map app: the map is the main experience, with filters or extra information beside it.

Or a document with an outline, an editor with properties.

Not all hinges are the same. Some foldables have a visible crease, but the display stays continuous. Others have a separating hinge, where the folding region creates distinct display areas.

And as multi-fold devices evolve, we may have several folding regions. That’s why we shouldn’t hardcode assumptions like “the hinge is always in the centre”. Android provides FoldingFeature information, including its bounds and whether it separates the display, and we should use it to make layout decisions.

Android isn’t just introducing new devices. It’s introducing new ways of using apps. Continuity means users can move between devices and carry on with their tasks. Bubbles bring floating app experiences.

Resizable windows mean users decide how much space an app gets. And the range of devices keeps expanding: square foldable displays, wider inner displays, tablets, desktops and emerging XR environments.

If our app assumes a single fixed layout, it’s going to struggle as these experiences become more common.

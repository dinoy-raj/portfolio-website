Adaptive Strategies to Specify

Reflow

The grid goes from one column to many

Reveal

Controls reflow and more content shows

Presentation Change

A bottom sheet becomes a side sheet

Shift & Rearrange

Elements move to new spots on the grid
⏱ 50s

Google’s own names, so designers can look them up, and each card shows it moving. Reflow: the grid expands from one column to many. Reveal: in control-heavy screens like a media player, the queue and extra controls appear as space grows.

Presentation change: a component changes form, here a bottom sheet becomes a side sheet. Shift and rearrange: the image, title, text and button move to new spots on the grid.

Panes can also move in, hide, expand, be constrained or pop up.

★ LAND: Every screen spec says which strategy applies at each size class.

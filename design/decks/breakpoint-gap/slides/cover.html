Training product designers for foldables, postures and multi-window UX

Dinoy Raj · droidCon India 2026 · Stage 4

⏱ 65s

Good afternoon everyone! Quick show of hands: how many of you here are Android developers? (pause)

Any product designers in the room? (pause)

Any PMs? (pause)

Honestly, my original plan was to scare the product designers and PMs into taking adaptive UI, hinge awareness and posture-driven layouts seriously.

Looking around, it seems most of you are engineers, which actually makes this even more important. Because making an app truly adaptive isn’t just a developer problem. As engineers, we already know the Compose APIs, the guidelines and the device constraints.

The real bottleneck is that for years, designers and product decision-makers have only thought in static phone frames. To ship great adaptive apps, we have to bridge that gap: by educating our design and product teams on how Android form factors have evolved, what constraints actually matter, and which design patterns and flows they need to spec from day one.

That’s exactly what we’re going to walk through today.

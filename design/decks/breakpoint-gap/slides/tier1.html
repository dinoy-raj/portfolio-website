At Tier 1, we start thinking about capabilities that are particularly useful on foldables and larger displays. First, posture-aware experiences. Then camera continuity, which matters when switching between the inner and outer displays.

We also have multitasking: picture-in-picture lets users keep watching or interacting with content while using another app, and interactive PiP goes one step further with custom controls. And finally, multi-instance lets users open several windows of the same app, for example to compare two documents side by side.

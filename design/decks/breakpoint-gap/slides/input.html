Designers spec focus and hover states, not just pressed

⏱ 35s

Another common mistake is assuming an Android app is always used through touch. Large-screen devices are frequently paired with keyboards, mice and styluses. So designers should specify how the experience works with each input method.

For keyboard users, we need a logical focus order. For mouse users, we need hover feedback and the right contextual interactions. For stylus users, we may need selection, drawing and handwriting support.

The main point: interaction design shouldn’t stop at defining touch behaviour.

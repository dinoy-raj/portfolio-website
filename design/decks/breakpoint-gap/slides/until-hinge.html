Stretch or Centre… Until the Hinge

Stretched: the Button Spans the Fold

Centred: the Whole Column Sits on the Hinge

Both pass every tier. Both put your most important content on the hinge.

⏱ 35s

Here’s a subtle design problem. Imagine a foldable display and a primary action button. If we stretch that button across the entire display, the hinge may pass right through the middle of it.

We might think centring the content solves the problem, but the whole column could still sit directly over the hinge. So the question isn’t simply whether our layout is centred or stretched.

We need to know where the hinge is, and make sure important content and interactions are positioned appropriately.

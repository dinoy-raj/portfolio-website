Hinge-Safe Checklist

01

Keep Actions Off the Hinge

02

Dialogs on One Side

03

No Text Across the Fold

04

Widen the Hinge Gutter
⏱ 25s

Here are four practical rules designers can apply. First, don’t put critical actions directly over the hinge. Second, position dialogs so they’re comfortable to read and interact with.

Third, avoid important text crossing the folding region. And finally, leave an appropriate gutter around the hinge when the display configuration requires it. These are simple decisions, but they make a significant difference to usability.

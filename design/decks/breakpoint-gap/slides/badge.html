Beyond the baseline requirements, Google is also creating an opportunity for apps that deliver better large-screen experiences. The Large Screen Badge recognises apps that go beyond simply running on a larger display.

To qualify, apps need to meet the Tier 2 and Tier 1 expectations. Then developers apply through the badge process and provide the right store listing assets. The benefit isn’t just technical compliance.

It’s a chance to show users the quality of the experience through Play Store merchandising.

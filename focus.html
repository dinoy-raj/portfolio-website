Pomodoro timer

Focus

A calm, distraction-free pomodoro timer. Plan your focus sessions, keep them on schedule, and see where your time goes, from the app, your home screen, the notification shade or a dynamic island around your camera.

SessionsStatisticsWidgetsDynamic island

Features

Pomodoro timer

Focus and break sessions that keep to schedule, with a calm, distraction-free timer screen.

Plan your sessions

Set up how long you focus and rest, and how many rounds you want to do.

Statistics

Weekly statistics show where your focus time goes, minute by minute.

Widgets

Start and follow a session from your home screen.

Notification shade

Pause, skip or stop without opening the app.

Dynamic island

A live timer around your camera cutout, always in view.

Your data

Your sessions and statistics are stored only on your phone.

No ads, no account and no analytics or tracking SDKs. You can remove all of the app's data at any time by clearing its storage or uninstalling it.

Permissions
  • Notificationsto show the running timer and tell you when a session ends
  • Display over other apps (optional)for the dynamic island
